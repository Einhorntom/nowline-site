NowLine

Your day at a glance on Windows. Press a shortcut, see what's next, press Esc and get back to work.

  • See today's events from all your Google accounts and calendars in one timeline.
  • Switch to an agenda list or a full-week view with one key.
  • Join a meeting, see who's coming and read the details without opening a browser.
  • Your Google Tasks that are due today or overdue, next to your events.
  • A reminder a few minutes before each meeting, with a Join button.
Private by design. NowLine only reads your calendars and tasks. Your data stays on your computer and is never sent to us or anyone else. Privacy policy
