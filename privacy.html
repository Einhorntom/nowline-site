Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

In short: NowLine reads your Google calendars and tasks so it can show them on your Windows computer. That data stays on your computer. We don't collect it, store it on servers, sell it or share it.

Who we are

NowLine is a Windows app developed by Tom Einhorn ("we"). Contact: einhorn.tom@gmail.com.

What NowLine accesses

When you connect a Google account, NowLine asks Google for read-only access to:

  • Your basic profile: your email address and name, to label the account in the app.
  • Your calendars and events: calendar names and colors, and event details such as title, time, location, description, meeting links, attendees and their responses.
  • Your Google Tasks: task lists and tasks, including titles, notes, due dates and subtasks.

NowLine cannot create, change or delete anything in your Google account.

How the data is used

Only to show your schedule and tasks inside NowLine on your computer, and to show reminders before events. Nothing else.

Where the data is stored

  • A copy of your calendars and tasks is saved only on your computer, in your Windows user's app data folder, so NowLine can open instantly and work offline.
  • The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored on your computer, encrypted with Windows' built-in protection for your Windows user.
  • NowLine talks directly to Google. Your data is never sent to us or to any other server.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your Google data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

NowLine's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Crash reports

Crash reporting is off by default. If you turn it on, NowLine sends technical information about errors: the app version, Windows version and where in the app the error happened. Crash reports never include event or task titles, descriptions, locations, attendees or any other calendar or task content.

Your control

  • Disconnect an account in NowLine's Settings to delete that account's saved data from your computer.
  • Uninstall NowLine to delete all of its data from your computer.
  • Revoke access at any time on your Google Account's Third-party apps & services page.

Children

NowLine is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
